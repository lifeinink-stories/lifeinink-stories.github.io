Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Life In Ink, India · lifeinink.stories@gmail.com

Life In Ink is an animated storytelling channel run by an individual creator. This policy covers (1) this website and (2) Life In Ink Studio, the private tool we use to publish our own videos and read our own channel statistics.

1. This website

This site is a set of static pages. It has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google Privacy Policy).

2. Life In Ink Studio (our publishing tool)

Life In Ink Studio is used only by the owner of the Life In Ink accounts, to manage those same accounts. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account or personal data. With the owner's permission it uses:

  • YouTube Data API (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to upload and schedule videos to the Life In Ink YouTube channel and to list that channel's videos and public statistics (views, likes, comment counts).
  • YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own performance reports (e.g. views, watch time, average view percentage, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Google Drive API (drive.file): to create a "Life In Ink" folder in the owner's Drive and upload finished videos there for review. With this scope the tool can only see files and folders it created itself, nothing else in the Drive.
  • Instagram API (Meta, via the Life In Ink Facebook Page): to publish Reels to the Life In Ink Instagram account and read that account's media and insights (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves).

What is stored and where. Access tokens and downloaded statistics are kept only on the owner's own computers (a laptop and a home Raspberry Pi), in files readable only by the owner. Statistics are aggregate numbers about our own videos. Comments, messages or personal details of viewers are not collected or stored.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through these APIs with anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. Data is transferred only to Google (YouTube, Drive) and Meta (Instagram) when the tool performs the action the owner requested, such as uploading a video.

Google API Services: Life In Ink Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube: Life In Ink Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

3. Retention, revoking access and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until access is revoked; analytics snapshots are kept to compare video performance over time and can be deleted at any time.
  • Revoke Google access: Google Account → Third-party apps & services → Life In Ink Studio → Remove access. See also Google's security settings.
  • Revoke Meta / Instagram access: Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites) → remove Life In Ink Studio.
  • Data deletion requests: email lifeinink.stories@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We delete any stored tokens and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

4. Our videos and social accounts

When you watch, like, comment on or message our videos on YouTube or Instagram, that data is handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies. Our stories are fictional; characters are drawn cartoons and do not depict real people.

5. Children

Our content and this site are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. Questions: lifeinink.stories@gmail.com.
