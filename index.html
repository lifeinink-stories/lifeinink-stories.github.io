Behind the ink

How a story is made

Small studio, careful process. Every video is written, drawn, voiced and checked before it goes out.

  1. 01

    Real moment

    It starts with something that happened to us or someone we know.

  2. 02

    Script & twist

    We find the twist and write it as a story worth telling, with a punchline.

  3. 03

    Draw & animate

    Original characters, sets, camera moves, music and sound, all in our ink style.

  4. 04

    Watch & post

    Every video is reviewed by a human before it's published. Then: your feed.
